Self-host Inter, Merriweather, and JetBrains Mono in `theme.css` so consumers get working fonts from one `@import "@sdwa/tokens/theme.css"`, with no `next/font`, Google Fonts `<link>`, or other per-framework font loader required. All three fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1; license text ships alongside the font files in `dist/fonts/{inter,merriweather,jetbrains-mono}/OFL.txt`.
